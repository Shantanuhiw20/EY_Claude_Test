## Accounts Payable Function Diagnostic
### Prepared for: Northwind Industrial Supplies Ltd.
### Prepared by: EY Finance Transformation

---

### 1. Executive Summary

Northwind's AP function processes roughly 8,200 invoices a month with a team of 9 FTEs. Compared to leading practice, the function is carrying meaningful hidden cost in two places: a high exception rate (18%, against a leading practice of under 5%) and low early-payment discount capture (32%, against a leading practice of over 80%). Days Payable Outstanding sits at 38 days, within the healthy range, so cash timing is not the issue. The issue is process friction before payment, not payment policy itself.

### 2. What We Heard

Across conversations with the AP team, three themes came up repeatedly:

- Invoices without a Purchase Order account for nearly 40% of volume and are the source of most exceptions.
- Approval routing has five steps for any invoice over ₹50,000, and two of those approvers are frequently unavailable for days at a time.
- The team is aware discounts are being missed but has no visibility into which vendors offer them or when they expire.

### 3. Volumetric Summary

| Metric | Northwind | Leading practice | Average | Read |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Invoices/month | 8,200 | - | - | Scale context |
| DPO | 38 days | 45-60 | 30-45 | Healthy |
| On-time payment rate | 89% | >95% | 85-95% | Average |
| Exception rate | 18% | <5% | 5-15% | Below average |
| Early discount capture | 32% | >80% | 50-80% | Below average |
| Invoice-to-posting cycle time | 6 days | <2 | 2-5 | Below average |
| PO-backed ratio | 61% | >80% | 50-80% | Average |

### 4. Diagnosis

The pattern points to a process design issue, not a people or technology issue. Non-PO invoices are both common and unmanaged, which cascades into the exception rate and the cycle time. The approval chain is longer than the invoice value typically warrants, and two roles are chronic bottlenecks. None of this shows up in the DPO number, which is why a single metric was never going to tell the full story, this is exactly why the conversation-based diagnostic matters.

### 5. Opportunities

1. **Reduce non-PO invoice volume.** Even capturing half of the current non-PO spend under a PO process would materially cut the exception rate.
2. **Simplify the approval chain for invoices under ₹2,00,000.** A three-step chain instead of five would recover several days of cycle time.
3. **Automate discount tracking.** A simple calendar of vendor discount terms, matched against the payment run, would recover a meaningful share of the 68% currently being missed.

### 6. Suggested Next Steps

- A two-week pilot simplifying approval routing for one business unit.
- A vendor-by-vendor discount capture tracker, reviewed monthly.
- A follow-up diagnostic in 90 days to re-measure the same metrics.
